YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Scope and who this policy covers

This policy describes information associated with the Giant website and Discord bot (the "Service"), how it may be used, and the choices available to you. Discord is a separate service and independently handles information under its own privacy policy. This policy describes Giant's current features; it will be updated if our practices change.

2. Information the Service may receive

Depending on how you use Giant and which permissions Discord grants, the bot may receive your Discord user ID, server ID, username, role or permission information, and command, interaction, or event content needed to perform a requested feature. If you contact support, the information you choose to include in that message may also be received.

The website may also be hosted by a provider that processes basic technical request information, such as IP address, browser details, and requested page, as part of delivering and protecting the site. The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive connection information when your browser requests those fonts.

3. Discord login

The optional login link requests Discord's identify, guilds, and email permissions and redirects back to the dashboard URL. The dashboard is currently a coming-soon page: Giant does not currently exchange the returned authorization code for a token, retrieve your profile or guild list, or provide an authenticated dashboard. Do not treat the current login link as an active account or sign-in feature.

4. How information is used

Information available to the Service may be used to operate bot commands and features, respond to support requests, maintain and troubleshoot the Service, protect against abuse or technical problems, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information to Giant.

5. When information may be shared

Information may be processed by Discord to provide the platform and bot integration, and by website or infrastructure providers needed to deliver and protect the Service. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the Service, or others. We do not sell personal information.

6. Cookies and similar storage

The current website does not create a Giant login session or use browser storage to authenticate you. Discord and hosting or other third-party services may use their own technologies when you visit or interact with them; their practices are governed by their own policies.

7. Retention and security

The specific information and retention period depend on the bot feature, operational needs, and the systems used to run the Service. We do not publish a fixed retention schedule at this time. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices and requests

You can remove Giant from your server or change its permissions using Discord, and you can revoke Discord authorizations through your Discord account settings. To ask a question or request help with information associated with Giant, contact the team through the support server. We may need enough information to understand and verify a request. Your rights may depend on the laws that apply where you live.

9. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone who is not permitted to use Discord under Discord's age requirements or the laws that apply to them. We do not knowingly seek to collect information from children who are not permitted to use the Service.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the Service or our practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with its updated date. For privacy questions or requests, contact the Giant team through the support server.
